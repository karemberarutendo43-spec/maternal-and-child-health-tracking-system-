# MCHTS Mother User Manual

## My Dashboard
- Login with your **Phone number + Password**.
- Use the dashboard to see your next ANC visit (if scheduled) and baby immunization status (given vs upcoming).
- If your account is not yet active, you may see a pending message until the clinic/admin approves.

## My Handbook
- Read pregnancy and baby-care information shared by the clinic/system.
- Use it as a quick reference between visits.

## Notifications
- Check notifications for clinic messages, approvals (like transfers), and important updates.

## My ANC Visits
- Review your ANC visit history and upcoming schedules (when available).
- Use it to confirm your next visit date and keep track of progress.

## My PNC Visits
- Review postnatal follow-up information after delivery (when available).
- Use it to understand upcoming follow-ups and what was recorded.

## Family Planning
- Review family planning information and recorded plans (where enabled by the clinic).
- If something looks wrong, report it to the clinic so they can correct it.

## Babies & Immunizations
- View your baby’s immunization schedule (upcoming vs given).
- Use it to know what vaccines are due next and which ones are overdue.

## Foods to Eat
- Use the food guidance section for nutrition suggestions during pregnancy and after delivery.
