# MCHTS Nurse User Manual

## Dashboard
- Login with **Username + Staff ID + Password**.
- Use the dashboard to confirm you’re in the correct clinic scope and to see quick counters (registrations, defaulters, queue items).
- If an admin reset your password, you’ll be forced to change it after login.

## Patients
In **Patients** you manage registration and searching:
- **Book Pregnancy**: register the mother and capture the key identifiers/contacts (this is the starting point for most cases).
- **Mother List**: view/search mothers registered in your clinic and open their profiles/history.
- **Register Baby**: after a mother is registered, register the baby and the system creates the immunization schedule.
- **Find Baby**: quick search to retrieve a baby record (use it when a mother arrives with a card/ID).

### Typical flow (most common)
1. Book Pregnancy (register mother)
2. Register Baby (create baby + schedule)
3. Use Mother List / Find Baby for follow-ups and quick retrieval

## Clinical
In **Clinical** you record services and review guidance:
- **Log PNC Visit**: record postnatal visits safely (correct date, correct mother).
- **PNC Schedule**: see upcoming/overdue PNC visits so you can plan follow-up.
- **Family Planning**: record FP method, start date, and changes (end previous method if switching).
- **Clinical Guidelines**: quick reference rules (e.g., no early completion without an override reason).
- **Baby Weights**: review weight entries and growth tracking where available.

## Queues
In **Queues** you handle items waiting for action:
- **Activation Queue**: see accounts that need activation/verification in your workflow.
- **Booking Queue**: review pending mother bookings and complete the next steps.
- **Transfer Requests**: track transfer requests and their status (pending/approved/rejected).

## Defaulters
In **Defaulters** you follow up missed services:
- **ANC Defaulters**: mothers overdue for ANC follow-up.
- **Immunization Defaulters**: babies overdue for doses.
- **Follow-up Tasks**: your working list for outreach and completion.

## Tools
In **Tools** you use supporting utilities:
- **Scan Baby Card**: fast retrieval from a baby card/identifier when available.
- **Stock (Supplies)**: view/update supply levels and identify items below reorder level.

## Monthly Reports
- Use **Monthly Reports** to generate and review the clinic’s monthly summaries for submission/supervision.
